    About

    I am an AI Researcher at KRAFTON. I like training foundation models, and I spend most of my time on what they should learn from and how to measure what they still cannot do. My goal is models that can see, listen, speak, and act as naturally as people do.

    Previously, I did my M.S. at the Graduate School of AI, KAIST, advised by Prof. Jinwoo Shin.

    News

    • 🔊 Our team at KRAFTON released Raon, a family of four multimodal models for speech understanding and generation. I worked on the data behind Raon-Speech-9B and Raon-SpeechChat-9B.
    • 📝 1 paper accepted to CVPR 2026 (See and Fix the Flaws).
    • 📝 2 papers accepted to ICLR 2026 (Orak, VLM-SubtleBench).
